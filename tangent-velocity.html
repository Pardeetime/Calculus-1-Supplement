A ball is dropped from the top of a tall building. After \( t \) seconds it has fallen \( s(t) = 4.9t^2 \) meters. How fast is it moving at exactly \( t = 2 \) seconds?

That sounds easy until you try it. Speed is distance divided by time, but at a single instant no time passes and no distance is covered. So measure the average velocity over shorter and shorter intervals starting at \( t = 2 \).

What you just did

You never computed a speed at an instant. You watched what the average speeds approach as the interval shrinks. That “approach” idea is called a limit, and it’s the reason this whole unit exists.

Average rate of change: slope of a secant line

The average rate of change of \( f \) from \( x = a \) to \( x = b \) is

\[ \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}. \]

On the graph, that’s the slope of the secant line through \( (a, f(a)) \) and \( (b, f(b)) \). For position, it’s average velocity. The units are always output units per input unit, like meters per second or dollars per item.

Instantaneous rate: slope of the tangent line

Write the second point as \( a + h \). Then the secant slope is

\[ m_{\text{sec}} = \frac{f(a+h) - f(a)}{h}. \]

As \( h \) shrinks toward 0, the secant line pivots toward the tangent line, the line that matches the curve’s direction at that point. The instantaneous rate of change is the value the secant slopes approach.

Why not just set h = 0?

Try it: \( \dfrac{f(a+0) - f(a)}{0} = \dfrac{0}{0} \), which has no meaning. We need a precise way to say “what the quotient approaches as \( h \) gets close to 0, without letting \( h \) equal 0.” That’s exactly what a limit is.

The algebra trick

For nice functions, you can simplify the secant slope before letting \( h \) shrink. Once the \( h \) in the denominator cancels, it’s easy to see where the expression is heading.

Where this goes

In Unit 2, the slope of the tangent line gets a name, the derivative \( f'(a) \), with the formal definition \( \displaystyle f'(a) = \lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h} \). You just did one by hand.
