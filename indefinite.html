A particle moves along a line with velocity \( v(t) = t^2 - 4t + 3 \) m/s for \( 0 \le t \le 5 \). It goes forward, turns around, and goes forward again. Press play and track two different totals: where it ends up compared with where it started, and how far it actually traveled.

Two different questions

Displacement (net change in position) is \( \int v\,dt \), where backward motion cancels forward motion. Total distance is \( \int |v|\,dt \), where every meter counts. They match only if the particle never reverses.

Because of the FTC, antiderivatives get integral notation. The indefinite integral

\[ \int f(x)\,dx = F(x) + C \quad\text{means}\quad F'(x) = f(x). \]

A definite integral \( \int_a^b \) is a number. An indefinite integral \( \int \) is a family of functions.

Net Change Theorem
\[ \int_a^b F'(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a) \]

The integral of a rate of change is the total change. Water flow rate → change in volume. Growth rate → change in population. Velocity → change in position.
