A graphing calculator plots points. Calculus finds the features, and together they force the shape of the graph. Turn on the layers one at a time, in order. Before you reveal the last one, try to picture the curve yourself.

Consistency check

Every feature has to agree. A graph can’t be increasing toward an asymptote from below while being concave up in a way that would cross it, for example. If your sketch contradicts your sign charts, one of them has an error, so recheck the derivative.

Problems 1–6 are all about \( f(x) = \dfrac{2x^2}{x^2 - 9} \). Useful facts: \( f'(x) = \dfrac{-36x}{(x^2-9)^2} \) and \( f''(x) = \dfrac{108(x^2 + 3)}{(x^2 - 9)^3} \).
