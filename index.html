How each lesson works

Four steps in every lesson

Every lesson follows the same four steps. Practice problems check your answer and offer up to three hints, but they don’t show the solution, so the work stays yours.

  1. 1

    See it

    Start with an interactive graph or model and notice what happens before you see any formula.

  2. 2

    Build it

    Turn the picture into notation one step at a time, with a “why does this work?” at every step.

  3. 3

    Try it

    Practice problems with three levels of hints: nudge, hint, then strategy. Your answer is checked, but the solution isn’t shown.

  4. 4

    Check yourself

    Rate your confidence so this dashboard can show you what to review before the exam.
